# Forget Less Privacy Policy

_Effective date: November 7, 2026_

Forget Less (on the App Store as "Forget Less: Daily Tasks") is a to-do app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app and its widgets.

**In short: Forget Less does not collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.**

## What the app stores

Forget Less stores the following on your device only:

- **Your tasks**: each task's name, the day it's planned for, its position in the list and whether it's complete.
- **Your settings**: your choices in the app, such as whether completed tasks are shown.

The app and its widgets share this data through storage on your device, so the widgets can show your tasks and let you complete or move them.

## What the app does not do

- It does not send your data to me or to anyone else. The app makes no network connections.
- It does not require an account or ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
- It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data.
- It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, calendars or anything else on your device.

## Data handled by Apple

Some data is handled by Apple's own services, under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), not by me:

- **Backups.** If you back up your iPhone or iPad to iCloud or to a computer, iOS or iPadOS may include Forget Less data in that backup.
- **Crash reports and usage statistics.** If you turned on "Share With App Developers" in your device's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage statistics with me. These do not include your tasks. I use them only to find and fix problems in the app.

## Widgets on the Lock Screen

If you add a Forget Less widget to your Lock Screen, its task names can be seen by anyone who looks at your iPhone or iPad, even while it is locked. You can remove the widget at any time.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a task in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your existing backups are kept until those backups are replaced or deleted.

## Children

Forget Less does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Forget Less handles your data differently, I will update this page before that version is released and change the effective date above.
