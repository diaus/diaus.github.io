# Forget Less Support

Forget Less (on the App Store as "Forget Less: Daily Tasks") is a to-do app for iPhone and iPad. You plan each task for a day, then complete it when it's done. This page answers common questions and tells you how to reach me.

**In short: if something doesn't work or you have a question, email me at [diaus.home@gmail.com](mailto:diaus.home@gmail.com) and I'll reply.**

## Contact

Email me at [diaus.home@gmail.com](mailto:diaus.home@gmail.com). I read every message, and you can write in English or Ukrainian.

If you're reporting a problem, these details help me fix it faster:

- your device model, such as iPhone 16 or iPad Air;
- your iOS or iPadOS version, from Settings → General → About;
- the app's version, from Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) → Forget Less;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if you can make one.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a widget?

Forget Less has one widget, "Upcoming", in four sizes: a Lock Screen widget and small, medium and large Home Screen widgets.

- **Home Screen:** touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Forget Less. Swipe to choose a size, then tap Add Widget.
- **Lock Screen:** touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then choose the Lock Screen. Tap the area below the clock, choose Forget Less, then tap Done.

To change a widget's settings, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

### What does the widget show?

It lists your incomplete tasks that are overdue or planned for today, in the same order as the app. Tasks for tomorrow follow them. A "!" marks an overdue task and a "→" marks a task for tomorrow. In the widget's settings, you can turn off "Show Overdue Tasks" or "Show Tomorrow's Tasks".

When the widget can't fit every task, it ends with a count such as "+3": the number of overdue and today's tasks it left out. Tomorrow's tasks are never counted. You can hide the count with "Show Left Out Count".

Tapping the widget opens the app. The widget updates after each change you make in the app, and at midnight.

### Can I complete or move tasks from the widget?

Yes, on the medium and large Home Screen widgets:

- Tap the circle in front of a task to complete it.
- Tap the up or down arrow at the end of a task to move it one step up or down. Moving down from the last task for today makes it the first task for tomorrow, as long as the widget lists tomorrow's tasks.

The widget never moves a task into or out of Overdue. To do that, use the app.

If you don't want the arrows, turn off "Show Move Arrows" in the widget's settings.

### I completed a task on the widget by mistake. Can I undo it?

Yes. A task you complete stays on the widget for a few seconds, crossed out. Tap its circle again to make it incomplete. Each medium and large widget has its own "Keep Complete Tasks For" setting: Off, 3, 5, 10 or 20 seconds. The default is 3 seconds.

### How do I move a task to another day?

In the app, any of these will do:

- Drag the task by the handle at the end of its row to any day in the list, or to another place on the same day.
- Tap the right half of the task's row to open a calendar beside it, then tap a day.
- Swipe the task to the left, tap the blue calendar button, choose a day and tap Done.
- Tap the task's name to edit it, then tap its day, such as "Today", and choose another.

### What are overdue tasks?

A task you didn't complete on its day becomes overdue. Overdue tasks are listed in the Overdue section above Today, oldest first, each with its own date in red. You can complete them, delete them or move them to today or a later day.

### Can I move all my overdue tasks at once?

Yes. Tap "Move All" in the Overdue header, then tap a day. Or touch and hold "Move All" to choose the day from a larger calendar, then tap Done. Every overdue task moves to the top of that day, in the order Overdue listed them.

### How do I hide completed tasks?

Tap the checklist button at the top of the list. Tap it again to show them. While completed tasks are hidden, a task you complete stays listed for a moment, crossed out, so you can undo a mis-tap. To change how long, tap the gear button and set "Keep Complete Tasks For".

### Can I see what I did on past days?

Yes. Tap the clock button at the top of the list to turn on History. History lists every day before today, with all of its tasks, completed or not. Scroll up to go further back, or tap the calendar button to go to a particular day. You can also add a task to a past day, or move a task back to one.

History is always off when the app starts.

### Can I use the app in Ukrainian?

Yes. Forget Less comes in English and Ukrainian, and follows your device's language.

### Are my tasks synced or backed up?

Your tasks are not synced between devices. They stay on the device where you entered them, and Forget Less has no account. If you back up your iPhone or iPad to iCloud or to a computer, your tasks can be included in that backup and restored from it. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/) for details.

### Can other people see my tasks on the Lock Screen?

Yes. A Lock Screen widget shows task names to anyone who looks at your device, even while it is locked. If that matters to you, keep the widget on the Home Screen instead, or remove it.

### How do I delete a task, or everything?

To delete a task, swipe it to the left, tap the trash button, and confirm. To delete all of your tasks and settings, delete the app. Copies in your existing backups are kept until those backups are replaced or deleted.
